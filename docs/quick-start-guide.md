# Using the Anti-Xeno Wiki

The Anti-Xeno Wiki is built as a fast, searchable static documentation hub designed for both desktop flight stations and mobile devices.

### Search
Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on macOS) anywhere on the site to open the full-text search modal. You can instantly jump to any guide, ship build, Thargoid variant, or tactic by keyword.

### Sidebar Navigation
The collapsible left sidebar organizes guides and documentation into structured categories:
- **General**: Intro, quick start, FAQ, and contribution guides.
- **Guides**: Pilot flight manuals, disengagement, and combat strategies.
- **Outfitting & Builds**: Recommended ship loadouts, shieldless theory, and weapon comparisons.
- **Combat Tactics**: Heart exertion, cold orbiting, and speedrunning.
- **Thargoids**: Interceptor intel, attack sequences, and location guides.
- **Engineering & Tools**: Material gathering, synthesis, and keybind setups.
- **Science & Research**: Frame data, damage mechanics, and research papers.

### Interactive Build Tabs
Many ship guides and weapon breakdowns feature interactive tabsets (`<AxiTabs>`). Click through the tabs at the top of a card to switch between flight platforms (such as Alliance Chieftain vs. Krait Mk.II) or weapon configurations without reloading the page.

### International Translations
The wiki is translated into multiple languages including German, French, Spanish, Italian, Russian, and Turkish. Use the **Languages** dropdown in the top navigation bar to switch your locale at any time.

# Getting Started Anti-Xeno Protocol

## Step 1 - Know Thy Enemy

If you are new to anti-xeno combat or operations, then the first priority should be to learn to identify the enemy. Head to [Thargoids](/thargoids) wiki page and pay attention to the "Thargoid Encounters" section. Come back here when ready for the next steps; setup and maneuvering.

### Know where you might find Thargoids

If you know where they are, you have some control over when you might encounter them.
View the [Finding Thargoids](/finding-thargoids) guide to learn more.

## Step 2 - Configuring Controls

AXI wiki presumes that its audience is most interested in combat. All wiki recommendations for controls and outfitting are going to have Thargoid combat as the basis of its advice. 

::: info
Your control scheme is important and affects every action you take in your ship. Optimize your settings for the best experience you can have.
:::


Visit [Recommended Controls](/recommended-controls) to get started with a control scheme that prioritizes precision aim. Your maneuvering should facilitate Time on Target (ToT) and your fine yaw and pitch controls become your bread and butter for bringing home the kills. Next up is maneuvering and then targeting.

## Step 3 - Learn to Fly

Thargoids that have engaged in combat are merciless. Survival is dependent on the pilot's ability to maneuver out of the way of incoming fire. Many strategies can be employed to great success. A variety of activities are covered in the [Flight Guide](/Flight). Regardless of the pilot's build strategy, an understanding and practice of [Cold Orbiting](/cold-orbiting) will assist to ensure the survival of the pilot during any Thargoid encounter.

### Learn to Disengage

Flight strategies should encompass getting into and out of combat with Thargoids. If you read the entire flight guide, you'll know this step 4 is a major section in the flight guide, [Disengagement](/Flight#disengagement).

## Step 4 - Learn to Fight

Once you know how to survive in the event of a Thargoid encounter, you gain options for going on the offensive. Visit [Basic Combat Guide](/basic-combat-guide) to get an introduction to a successful offensive strategy to combat with Thargoids.

What comes next is up to you! Curiosity is your best friend to learning all about Thargoids and Elite. If there is anything in particular you find yourself curious about and cannot find it by searching the AXI wiki, try communicating in the AXI discord. With over 27,000 members, the AXI discord is very active and welcoming!
